## English – Full description
Inherit Grandma Rosa's beloved wedding hall and turn it into the most famous wedding empire in town!

Rosa ran The Rose Hall for forty years. Now it's tired, the kitchen is old, the bank wants its money, and Victor Crane from the Grand Palace across the street wants to buy it for almost nothing. Can you bring it back to life?

💍 MEET COUPLES AND BOOK WEDDINGS
Every couple has a love story, a personality and a dream. Listen to their wishes, send the invoice and set your price. Too high and they walk away; too low and you lose money.

🎨 DESIGN DREAM WEDDINGS
Choose from 8 themes: Classic, Royal Gold, Garden Boho, Beach, Modern Glam, Romantic, Rustic and Fairytale. Pick the flowers, lighting, music, food and seating, and watch your hall transform with new centerpieces, decor, string lights and flower arches.

✏️ DESIGN STUDIO
Arrange your hall on a beautiful floor plan: tables, dance floor, stage, bar, buffet, cake, flower arches, fountains and more. Then see it all in 3D.

🎉 RUN THE BIG NIGHT LIVE
The couple arrives by limousine, petals fall, the first dance begins... and then the rings go missing! Fix problems in time, serve the tables, answer special requests from the couple and tap the golden ring at the perfect moment for the cake, the first dance and the bouquet toss.

👥 HIRE YOUR DREAM TEAM
Waiters, cooks, decorators, DJs, security, valets, hosts and event managers. Each one has skills, a personality, morale and even friendships and rivalries.

🏛️ GROW YOUR EMPIRE
Upgrade your hall, open a restaurant, hotel, garden venue and pool terrace, run marketing campaigns, research new ideas and buy up to six stunning halls: mountain lodge, sky-high ballroom, desert palace, lakeside garden and a historic castle.

⚔️ BEAT VICTOR CRANE
Your rival steals clients, upgrades his palace and taunts you. Compare your halls side by side and win back the town!

✨ AND MUCH MORE
• 12 story chapters with surprises and rewards
• Customize your owner, with unlockable outfits and cars
• A portfolio of every wedding, with photos and reviews
• Your own wedding social app to gain followers
• Day and night cycle, weather and cinematic wedding moments
• Weekly goals, achievements, a daily gift and Legacy mode
• Fully playable in English and Arabic
• Plays offline
