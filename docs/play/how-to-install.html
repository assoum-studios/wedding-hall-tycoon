Play on iPhone and keep your progress

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Always open the game from the new Wedding Hall Tycoon icon on your home screen.

Your game saves automatically on your phone. Opened from the home screen, Safari keeps your saves. Opened as a normal web page, Safari may delete them after about a week without playing.

Extra safety: in the game open Office → ⚙ Settings → Save backup → Copy code and keep the code in Notes. Use Load a backup to restore it on any phone.

Use headphones or turn off silent mode to hear the music. Ads and purchases are only in the Android app.

العب على الآيفون واحتفظ بتقدّمك

  1. افتح هذه الصفحة في سفاري.
  2. اضغط زر المشاركة (المربع مع السهم).
  3. اختر إضافة إلى الشاشة الرئيسية ثم إضافة.
